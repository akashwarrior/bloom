# Interface craft

Adapted from Jakub Krehel's [Better Layout, Better Typography, Better Writing, Better Colors and Better UI](https://jakub.kr/skills).

On a new build the direction decides the arrangement; these apply inside each region and wherever the direction is silent. On a focused edit, keep the existing arrangement.

- Group related content with smaller internal gaps and more space between groups. Share alignment edges. Use panels or dividers where they convey a real boundary. Break the layout where its content stops fitting, let rows grow with their labels, and place the primary action where it stays reachable. Give collapsed content a visible, descriptive control.
- Give headings, body copy, labels and data a small, consistent set of type roles. Pair each role with a line height that suits its typical wrapping. Keep long prose at a readable measure. Where important text is clipped, provide a way to see the full value by keyboard or touch.
- Use the product's own vocabulary. Place errors beside the affected input and give people a concrete way to recover. Use consistent terms in pending and completed states.
- Give the primary action a clear visual lead and pair status color with text or an icon. Check contrast on the actual surface when applying text over artwork or transparency.
- Keep icons consistent in weight and legible at their rendered size. Use borders for structure and depth only where it clarifies which surface is above another. Where two rounded surfaces nest with even spacing, make their curves follow the same contour.
